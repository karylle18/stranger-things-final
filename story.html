The story so far

Four seasons in Hawkins, kept spoiler-light.

Season 1

November 1983 (released 2016)

Will Byers vanishes on his way home. His friends meet a girl with a shaved head and strange abilities, while Chief Hopper and Joyce uncover the secret behind Hawkins Lab and a gate to another world.

Season 2

Autumn 1984 (released 2017)

Will keeps seeing visions of the Upside Down. Max arrives in Hawkins, Dustin brings home a strange pet, and a huge shadow creature spreads below the town.

Season 3

Summer 1985 (released 2019)

The new Starcourt Mall is the center of town, and something dangerous is hiding beneath it. The group splits into teams to find out what the Russians are building.

Season 4

Spring 1986 (released 2022)

The friends are scattered across the country. A new villain targets teenagers one by one, and the Party has to work together across three storylines to stop it.
