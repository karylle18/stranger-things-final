Places in Hawkins

Six locations that matter to the story.

Hawkins National Laboratory

A government facility on the edge of town where Eleven was kept. It holds the secret of the gate to the Upside Down.

Starcourt Mall

The three-level shopping center that opens in Season 3. It is where Steve, Dustin and others work, shop and uncover secrets.

Hopper's cabin

A hidden cabin in the woods where Hopper lives and where Eleven hides in the early seasons.

The Byers house

Home to Joyce, Will and Jonathan. The wall covered in lights and letters is its most famous feature.

The Wheeler basement

Mike's basement is the Party's headquarters, home to their Dungeons & Dragons campaigns.

Scoops Ahoy

The sailor-themed ice cream shop at Starcourt Mall where Steve and Robin work in Season 3.
