Jim Hopper

Hawkins' worn-out police chief with a soft spot he hides badly.

Who they are

Jim Hopper is the chief of police in Hawkins. He moved back from New York after losing his young daughter, and he deals with the grief by keeping to himself, drinking coffee and ignoring paperwork.

When Will Byers vanishes, he stops brushing the case aside and follows it all the way to the lab. He ends up hiding Eleven in his cabin and learning to be a parent again.

Personality

  • Stubborn
  • Protective
  • Dry sense of humor

Relationships

  • Eleven: the girl he takes in and raises
  • Joyce Byers: ally, sparring partner and friend
  • Officer Callahan and Officer Powell: his deputies
Show spoiler note

At the end of Season 3 he is believed dead. Season 4 reveals what really happened to him.
