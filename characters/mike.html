Mike Wheeler

The Dungeon Master who keeps the Party together.

Who they are

Mike is the planner of the group. He runs their Dungeons & Dragons campaigns from his basement and treats every problem in Hawkins like a quest that needs a strategy.

He is the first to shelter Eleven, and his loyalty to her and to his friends drives many of his choices, sometimes bravely and sometimes stubbornly.

Personality

  • Natural leader
  • Loyal
  • Stubborn when worried

Relationships

  • Eleven: the friend he hides in his basement
  • Dustin Henderson: best friend and Party member
  • Will Byers: best friend he never stops searching for
  • Nancy Wheeler: his older sister
Show spoiler note

His bond with Eleven is tested in Season 4 when they spend most of it on different sides of the country.
