Dustin Henderson

The curious, cap-wearing inventor with a big laugh and bigger ideas.

Who they are

Dustin is the talker of the Party and the one most likely to build a gadget out of spare parts. Radios, compasses and homemade experiments are how he makes sense of the weird events in Hawkins.

He is endlessly curious, which gets him into trouble, such as when he adopts a strange creature he finds in a trash can. He is also the friend who checks on everyone else.

Personality

  • Curious
  • Funny
  • Great with tech

Relationships

  • Mike Wheeler: best friend and fellow Party member
  • Steve Harrington: unlikely mentor and friend
  • Will Byers: Party member and friend
  • Suzie: his long-distance girlfriend
Show spoiler note

In Season 4 he joins the Hellfire Club and forms a friendship with its leader, Eddie.
