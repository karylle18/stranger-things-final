Will Byers

The boy who was lost in the Upside Down and came back changed.

Who they are

Will is a quiet, artistic kid who loves drawing and playing D&D with his friends. His disappearance in Season 1 is what sets the whole story in motion.

After he returns, he stays connected to the Upside Down in ways nobody else understands. That link makes him a warning system for the Party, even when it frightens him.

Personality

  • Gentle
  • Creative
  • Quietly resilient

Relationships

  • Joyce Byers: his determined mother
  • Mike Wheeler: closest friend
  • Eleven: friend who helped bring him home
  • Jonathan Byers: his protective older brother
Show spoiler note

His link to the Mind Flayer is the main plot of Season 2, and it continues to shape how he sees Hawkins.
