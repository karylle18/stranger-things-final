Eleven

A quiet girl from a secret lab whose mind can move the world.

Who they are

Eleven is found in the woods outside Hawkins after escaping Hawkins National Laboratory, where she was raised as a test subject. She speaks very little at first, and the Party teaches her what friendship, jokes and snacks are.

Her mind can move objects, read distant places and open the way into the Upside Down. Using her power at full strength leaves her exhausted, and her nose bleeds when she pushes too far.

Personality

  • Brave under pressure
  • Fiercely loyal
  • Learns fast, trusts slowly

Relationships

  • Mike Wheeler: first friend and closest companion
  • Jim Hopper: guardian who becomes her adoptive father
  • Max Mayfield: friend who shows her life outside the cabin
  • Dr. Martin Brenner: the lab scientist she calls Papa
Show spoiler note

Season 4 takes her powers away for a long stretch, and she has to face the memories of the lab to get them back.
