Max Mayfield

The skateboarding newcomer who beats the boys at their own arcade game.

Who they are

Max arrives in Hawkins in Season 2 with her mom and stepbrother Billy. She is sharp, independent and not shy about taking the top score on Dustin's favorite arcade game.

She is wary at first, but she becomes a loyal member of the Party and one of Eleven's closest friends.

Personality

  • Independent
  • Competitive
  • Guarded but caring

Relationships

  • Eleven: close friend
  • Dustin Henderson: arcade rival turned friend
  • Steve Harrington: babysitter-style ally
  • Billy Hargrove: her stepbrother
Show spoiler note

In Season 4 she becomes Vecna's target, and a favorite song helps her hold on.
