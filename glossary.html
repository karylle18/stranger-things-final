The Hawkins Glossary

Find definitions for important places, creatures, objects and groups in Stranger Things. Content covers Seasons 1–4, so some entries may contain spoilers.


Object

Christmas lights

Lights Joyce Byers uses as a possible way to communicate with Will in Season 1.

Creature

Demogorgon

A predatory creature from the Upside Down that first threatens Hawkins in Season 1.

Game

Dungeons & Dragons (D&D)

A fantasy tabletop role-playing game played by the boys; they borrow creature names from it to describe supernatural threats.

Dimension

The Gate

An opening that connects the human world with the Upside Down.

Location

Hawkins

The fictional Indiana town where many key events of the series take place.

Location

Hawkins National Laboratory

A research facility tied to experiments and unusual events in Hawkins.

Group

Hellfire Club

A Dungeons & Dragons club at Hawkins High School, associated with Eddie Munson in Season 4.

Creature

The Mind Flayer

A powerful entity linked to the threats emerging from the Upside Down.

Group

The Party

The group of friends who frequently use D&D language and work together against supernatural dangers.

Location

Rainbow Room

A room associated with the young test subjects at Hawkins National Laboratory.

Location

Starcourt Mall

A shopping mall in Hawkins that becomes central to the events of Season 3.

Dimension

Upside Down

A dark, dangerous alternate dimension that resembles Hawkins and contains hostile creatures.

Creature

Vecna

A major supernatural antagonist introduced in Season 4 who targets people through psychological trauma.

Object

Walkie-talkie

A handheld radio the group uses to communicate and coordinate during adventures.
