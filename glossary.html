Glossary

Terms and creatures you will run into.

The Upside Down

A dark mirror version of Hawkins, covered in vines and floating spores.

The Demogorgon

The creature that takes Will in Season 1. Mike's group named it after a Dungeons & Dragons monster.

The Mind Flayer

A giant shadow monster that controls people and creatures from the Upside Down.

Vecna

The main villain of Season 4, who attacks teenagers through their worst memories.

The Party

The name for Mike, Dustin, Will, Eleven, Max and Lucas, the friends who face each threat together.

Hellfire Club

The Hawkins High Dungeons & Dragons group that Dustin joins in Season 4.
